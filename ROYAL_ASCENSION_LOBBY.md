# The Royal Ascension Lobby

The Royal Ascension is the premium social lobby for Crown Chaos. It is a floating corrupted royal fortress suspended above an abyss, designed to make players feel like they are entering a serious competitive world built around status, spectacle, and becoming king.

## Emotional Goals

- This game is serious.
- This world is huge.
- I want to become the king.
- Everything here feels expensive.

## Core Structure

Implemented in `src/server/08_WorkspaceSetup.server.lua` as `TheRoyalAscension_Lobby`.

## Shop Cathedral

The shop is not a bright marketplace. It should feel like a dangerous sacred artifact vault.

Implemented elements:

- Circular chamber
- Floating item displays
- Rotating showcase swords
- Rarity color lights
- Legendary center column
- Dark walls with gold trim

Rarity colors:

- Common: `#6F6F76`
- Rare: `#3A7BFF`
- Epic: `#A53DFF`
- Legendary: `#FFB547`
- Corrupted Mythic: `#FF2EFF`

## Guild Hall

The Guild Hall should feel like a royal war chamber, not a normal Roblox room.

Implemented elements:

- Guild leaderboard wall
- Throne section
- Trophy displays
- Duel circle
- Giant pillars
- Warm gold and corruption lighting

Future expansion:

- Clan creation table
- Guild banners loaded from clan identity
- Private strategy room teleport pads
- Guild trophy unlocks

## Social Design

The lobby should encourage:

- Flexing cosmetics
- Waiting with friends
- Recording clips
- Showing founder status
- Trying emotes
- Inspecting shop items
- Hype before matches

Important rule:

Avoid simulator clutter. No random bright kiosks. Every feature should look like part of the royal fortress.

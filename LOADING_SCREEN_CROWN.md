## Design Direction

The crown is meant to be the first loading-screen hero object for Crown Chaos: a broken royal artifact waking up before the match starts.

Core reads:

- Dark fantasy royal crown, not a bright simulator pickup.
- Aged gold, black iron, void-black recesses, and purple corruption energy.
- Large readable silhouette from far away.
- Gothic plate teeth instead of simple cones.
- Recessed black plaques, rim beads, thorn silhouettes, broken metal fragments, and corruption veins.
- Floating chains, shards, ritual rings, and a vertical summoning beam for loading-screen motion.

## Next Manual Polish Pass

This procedural Blender pass establishes the direction, layout, and usable file. The next true art pass should focus on:

- Hand-shaping the crown teeth so they feel less procedural.
- Replacing some simple flat plaques with sculpted filigree.
- Adding UV-ready trim-sheet materials for Roblox SurfaceAppearance.
- Separating the clean crown mesh from render-only loading-screen props such as text, backdrop, and ritual rings.
